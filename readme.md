It serves as a home for original study notes, projects, and reflections connecting technical delivery with product strategy, customer needs, and measurable business outcomes.

## About Me

I’m Brian Walsh, an Implementation Technical Lead at ADP with experience leading 10+ concurrent enterprise software implementations, resolving complex technical escalations, and guiding teams through testing, go-live, and post-launch support. My background includes customer training, technical documentation, mentoring, and working with leadership and development teams to address customer needs.

## Program Overview

The program combines technical expertise with strategic management skills through a competency-based approach designed for working professionals. The Product Management concentration emphasizes how technology products are developed, delivered, and improved throughout their life cycle.

### Key Areas of Study

| Focus Area | Learning Emphasis |
| --- | --- |
| Product strategy and life cycle management | Taking a technology product from idea through launch and scaling. |
| Customer-focused innovation | Using market research and user feedback to guide product innovation and development. |
| Agile and lean methodologies | Applying frameworks that help coordinate cross-functional teams and improve delivery. |
| Data-driven decision-making | Using analytics and business intelligence to inform product direction and evaluate outcomes. |
